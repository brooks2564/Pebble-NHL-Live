# NHL Live Scores 🏒

Live NHL scores on your Pebble. Pick a team, glance at your wrist.

## Features

- **Team logos** on color watches (Emery, Basalt), colored abbreviations elsewhere
- **Live score**, period and clock, shots on goal, last goal scorer
- **Power play line** - team logo, skater count (5v4) and penalty time left
- **Goal celebration** - animated goal light and vibration when your team scores
- **Records or playoff series status** for both teams
- **Next game** when your team isn't playing
- **League ticker** of other games, with adjustable speed
- **Battery bar** (optional)

## Settings

Team, vibrate on goal, battery bar, team logos, timezone, ticker speed.

## Platforms

Aplite, Basalt, Diorite, Emery, Flint
